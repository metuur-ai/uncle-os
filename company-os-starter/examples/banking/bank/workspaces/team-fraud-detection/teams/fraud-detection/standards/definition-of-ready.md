## Team Definition of Ready
- The problem is stated in Fraud vocabulary (Transaction, Alert, Case,
  Disposition — see context://fraud).
- Expected effect on false-negative rate is stated.
- Affected components and consumed platform clauses are identified.
- Acceptance criteria are testable.
