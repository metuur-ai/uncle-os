# Developer onboarding (bank)

Company-wide orientation: governance tiers (mandatory / default / guidance),
the change lifecycle (discovery -> PRD -> reality update), data-residency as
the non-negotiable outermost control, and how your team workspace materializes
platform slices via `workspace sync`. Domain vocabulary is owned by the
ontology — check your bounded context before naming things ("Transaction"
means different things in Payments and Fraud).
